# Battle sound effects

The short battle sounds are bundled locally so combat audio works offline.

- `fire.ogg` is converted from **Fire & Evil Spell** by artisticdude (submitted by qubodup), licensed CC BY 3.0: https://opengameart.org/content/fire-evil-spell
- `earth.ogg` is **Earth Element Magic Spell** by qubodup, licensed CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/earth-element-magic-spell
- `water.ogg` is `splash_03.ogg` from **40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX** by rubberduck, licensed CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
- `ultimate_charge.ogg` is `magical_1.ogg` from **Magic Spell SFX** by JaggedStone, licensed CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/magic-spell-sfx
- `wind.ogg` is `knifeSlice.ogg` from **RPG Audio** by Kenney, licensed CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- `punch.ogg` and `leaf.ogg` are impact sounds from **Impact Sounds** by Kenney, licensed CC0: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- `ultimate_explosion.ogg` is a 0.9-second cut from **Sharp Explosion Weird_Natural_reverb** by Rudmer_Rotteveel, licensed CC0: https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/336009/

Attribution for the CC BY 3.0 fire sound: “Fire & Evil Spell” by artisticdude, submitted by qubodup, https://opengameart.org/content/fire-evil-spell, CC BY 3.0.
