Cozy Farm SFX — 55 soft sound effects for farming and life-sim games

Tools, harvest, items, shop, world, UI, jingles and seamless ambience loops, with a soft,
natural sound that fits cozy games.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit. Sound effects are mono and start and end in silence; the
ambience loops are stereo and repeat with no gap or click (set your player to loop).
