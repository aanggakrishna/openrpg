# GBA environment assets

Generated by `tools/build_gba_environment.py` from the local
`artifacts/firered-rev1-assets` pret/pokefirered study export. These are FireRed
graphics, not a claim of direct extraction from the user's ROM or the Renoval
ROM hack. Pokemon artwork is deliberately **not** loaded from this directory:
the game uses its previous PokéAPI cache and animated battle sprites.

The compiler applies the original metatile layers, palette indices, flip bits,
and map block layout before exporting complete buildings and trees. Runtime
does not require the source export, a ROM, or an internet connection for scenery.

Covered: terrain, paths, water, trees, rocks, houses, service buildings, interior
tiles/furniture and battle terrain. Custom farm animals, gameplay effects and
saved home decorations without a corresponding GBA asset retain their existing
renderers. Interface panels are code-rendered in the new field-journal palette.

Pokemon/FireRed artwork remains the property of its respective rights holders;
these exports are for the user's local study project, not a redistribution license.
