# Everyday action sounds

The bundled cozy farming, home, and interface effects come from **Cozy Farm SFX** by Bramble & Byte, licensed CC BY 4.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/cozy-farm-sfx-50-farming-game-sound-effects-tools-harvest-shop-ui-jingles. The included `COZY-FARM-LICENSE.txt` and `COZY-FARM-README.txt` are from the source package.

`chicken.ogg` is **Chicken Sound Effect** by IMadeIt, licensed CC BY 3.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/chicken-sound-effect. The source license is included as `CHICKEN-LICENSE.txt`.

`roar_01.ogg`, `roar_02.ogg`, `hurt_01.ogg`, `hurt_02.ogg`, `grunt_01.ogg`, and `grunt_02.ogg` are from **80 CC0 creature SFX** by rubberduck, released under CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx.

`cook-sizzle.wav` is a short procedural sound effect generated for OpenRPG.

`stove-on.ogg` is `turn_stove_on_01.ogg` from **stove switch** by TinyWorlds, released under CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/stove-switch.
