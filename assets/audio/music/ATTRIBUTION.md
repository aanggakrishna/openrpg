## CC0 sanctuary and battle collection

The following files in `cc0_rpg/` are from **“15 Melodic RPG Chiptunes”** by
Aureolus_Omicron, licensed **CC0**. They are selected by sanctuary biome and
zone, so each sanctuary has a stable but varied loop. The two battle tracks are
also available to the random battle music selector.

- `rpgchip03_town.ogg` — Town
- `rpgchip06_dungeon.ogg` — Dungeon
- `rpgchip07_the_shrine_of_mysteries.ogg` — Shrine of Mysteries
- `rpgchip09_rainy_streets.ogg` — Rainy Streets
- `rpgchip10_fountain_of_the_fairies.ogg` — Fountain of the Fairies
- `rpgchip11_airship.ogg` — Airship
- `rpgchip13_battle_1.ogg` — Battle 1
- `rpgchip14_battle_2.ogg` — Battle 2

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/15-melodic-rpg-chiptunes
